# Introduction
A manager at the bank is disturbed with more and more customers leaving their credit card services. They would really appreciate if one could predict for them who is gonna get churned so they can proactively go to the customer to provide them better services and turn customers' decisions in the opposite direction.

## Tools I Used
- The dataset came from https://leaps.analyttica.com/home

- **SQL** : The programming language used to query the database

- **PostgreSQL** : The database management system used

- **Git & GitHub**: Used to store and track projects

- **Power BI**: Turned my findings into charts to share and visualize the data to highlight my findings

## Analysis
1) Existing customers, on average, had a higher credit limit (close to about $600 more) than attrited customers.

2) Existing customers, on average, had owned more credit cards from the company compared to attrited customers.

3) Attrited customers, on average, had contacted the credit card company more times than existing customers.

4) Existing customers, on average, had more (about twice as much) revolving balance on their credit card compared to attrited customers.
